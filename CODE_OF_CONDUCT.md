# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully and constructively.
Examples of encouraged behavior include:

- Being clear, factual, and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing disagreement on technical details and project goals.
- Respecting different levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, publishing private information without consent, or sustained
disruption of project discussions.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict
participation when behavior is inconsistent with this code of conduct.
